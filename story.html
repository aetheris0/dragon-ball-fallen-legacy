Across countless worlds, timelines, and stories, the idea of an ending has always marked the limit of what comes next. But what happens when a being exists beyond the very meaning of an ending?

At the center of Fallen Legacy stands Goku: a figure surrounded by violet flame and accompanied by the image of a colossal shadow dragon. His legend is built around one impossible principle — finality itself.
